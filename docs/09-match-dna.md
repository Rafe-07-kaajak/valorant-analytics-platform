# Match DNA

Version: 1.0

Status: LOCKED

---

# Purpose

Match DNA is the signature experience of the platform.

It transforms hundreds of statistics into a visual identity that allows users to understand how a team actually plays.

Instead of asking:

"Which team is stronger?"

the platform answers:

"How do these two teams naturally interact?"

Match DNA is designed to become the most memorable feature of the project.

---

# Product Goal

The user should immediately understand that every team has its own identity.

Instead of showing dozens of unrelated numbers, Match DNA compresses all important information into a single visual representation.

The feature should make comparison feel intuitive rather than analytical.

---

# User Story

A user selects Team A and Team B.

The platform analyzes both teams.

A DNA profile is generated for each side.

The user immediately understands:

- why the prediction favors one team
- how both teams are similar
- where they differ
- which characteristics decide the match

The user should never need to inspect raw statistics to reach these conclusions.

---

# Core Philosophy

Prediction explains the result.

Match DNA explains the reason.

The prediction engine answers:

"Who wins?"

Match DNA answers:

"Why?"

---

# DNA Dimensions

Each team is represented by multiple behavioral dimensions.

Examples include:

- Aggression
- Tempo
- Map Control
- Utility Efficiency
- Adaptability
- Economy Discipline
- Clutch Ability
- Entry Success
- Defensive Stability
- Mid Round Decision Making

These dimensions are product concepts rather than raw statistics.

The prediction engine converts statistics into these higher-level traits.

---

# Visual Representation

Each team receives its own DNA profile.

The profile should feel alive.

Possible visual language:

- flowing strands
- connected nodes
- animated curves
- radar-like structures
- dynamic ribbons

The visualization should never resemble a spreadsheet.

It should communicate personality.

---

# Comparison Experience

After selecting two teams:

Step 1

Generate Team A DNA.

Step 2

Generate Team B DNA.

Step 3

Animate both profiles toward each other.

Step 4

Highlight common traits.

Step 5

Highlight conflicting traits.

Step 6

Display similarity score.

Step 7

Reveal the final prediction.

---

# Similarity Score

The platform produces a similarity value.

Example:

82%

This value measures playstyle similarity rather than team strength.

Two weak teams can still have high similarity.

Two elite teams can have very different identities.

---

# Interaction

Hovering over any DNA trait reveals:

- explanation
- influence on prediction
- supporting evidence

The user should be encouraged to explore rather than simply consume information.

---

# Motion Design

Animations should communicate reasoning.

DNA profiles slowly assemble.

Traits grow naturally.

Connections appear progressively.

Comparison lines animate from one team to another.

Nothing should appear instantly.

Motion should reinforce understanding.

---

# Emotional Goal

The user should feel:

"I've never seen match analysis presented like this before."

This emotional response is more important than showing additional statistics.

---

# Design Principles

Prioritize:

- clarity
- motion
- storytelling
- interaction

Avoid:

- clutter
- unnecessary numbers
- static charts
- spreadsheet aesthetics

---

# Future Extensions

Future versions may include:

- historical DNA evolution
- tournament meta comparison
- player DNA

- regional DNA

These extensions are outside Version 1.
