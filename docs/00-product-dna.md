# Product Principles

These principles are permanent.

They take priority over features, technology choices, and implementation details.

## 1. Insight Before Prediction

The product does not exist to predict match winners.

It exists to help users understand *why* a prediction is made.

Prediction is the entry point.

Insight is the product.

---

## 2. Every Prediction Must Be Explainable

Every probability shown to the user must be backed by evidence.

Every prediction must answer:

- Why?
- Which factors matter?
- How much does each factor contribute?

Black-box predictions are not acceptable.

---

## 3. Users Control Assumptions

Predictions are not absolute truths.

Users should be able to modify assumptions such as:

- Series format
- Selected maps
- Recent form weighting
- Head-to-head weighting

The system immediately reflects how these changes affect the prediction.

The platform encourages exploration rather than passive consumption.

---

## 4. Professional Play Only

The platform focuses exclusively on professional Tier-1 Valorant.

Supported competitions include:

- VCT Americas
- VCT EMEA
- VCT Pacific
- VCT China
- Masters
- Champions

The platform does not support:

- Ranked
- Premier
- Tier-2
- Community tournaments

---

## 5. Engineering Before Features

A small number of polished features is better than a large number of unfinished features.

The platform values:

- reliability
- maintainability
- explainability
- responsiveness

over feature quantity.

---

# Vision

Build the most trustworthy competitive intelligence platform for professional Valorant.

The platform should become a place where users can explore, understand, and experiment with professional match predictions instead of simply viewing probabilities.

---

# Mission

Transform competitive Valorant data into understandable competitive knowledge.

The platform should reduce the gap between raw statistics and human understanding.

---

# Target Users

Primary users

- Competitive Valorant fans
- Esports analysts
- Amateur coaches
- Content creators
- Students interested in esports analytics

Secondary users

- Data science learners
- Machine learning enthusiasts
- Recruiters reviewing engineering portfolios

---

# Core Value Proposition

Most prediction websites answer one question.

"Who is likely to win?"

This platform answers four.

- Who is likely to win?
- Why?
- Which assumptions matter?
- How would the prediction change if the assumptions changed?

---

# Competitive Advantages

## Explainable Prediction

Predictions are always accompanied by evidence.

Users understand the reasoning behind every probability.

---

## Interactive Prediction Studio

Users can modify prediction assumptions and observe changes immediately.

The prediction engine becomes interactive rather than static.

---

## Team DNA

Every professional team is represented through a generated competitive identity rather than a simple collection of statistics.

---

## Competitive Intelligence

The platform connects statistics, engineered features, simulations, and explanations into one coherent experience.

---

# Product Scope

Version 1 focuses on one experience.

Prediction Studio.

Everything else exists only to support Prediction Studio.

---

# Non Goals

The platform intentionally does NOT include:

- Betting advice
- Gambling features
- Fantasy esports
- Social networking
- Community discussions
- User-generated rankings
- Live in-match prediction
- Player trading
- Login systems
- Paid subscriptions

These features are outside the scope of Version 1.

---

# Success Metrics

The product is considered successful when users can:

- understand predictions,
- trust predictions,
- experiment with assumptions,
- learn something new after every interaction.

Accuracy alone is not considered success.

Understanding is.
