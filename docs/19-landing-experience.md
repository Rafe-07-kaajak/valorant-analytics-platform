# Section Timing

Each section should earn the user's attention before asking for the next interaction.

The landing page should maintain a steady rhythm that feels intentional rather than rushed.

Every section has a different responsibility and therefore deserves a different amount of attention.

---

## Hero

Expected engagement:

3–5 seconds

Goal:

Capture attention immediately.

Users should understand the product category, its value, and the primary action without scrolling.

---

## Product Story

Expected engagement:

10–15 seconds

Goal:

Explain why the product exists.

Focus on the problem before introducing the solution.

---

## Core Features

Expected engagement:

15–20 seconds

Goal:

Demonstrate the platform's unique capabilities.

Each feature should communicate one key idea.

Avoid presenting multiple concepts simultaneously.

---

## Prediction Studio Preview

Expected engagement:

20–30 seconds

Goal:

Generate excitement.

Users should begin imagining themselves using the product.

This section should become the emotional peak of the landing page.

---

## Technology Section

Expected engagement:

10–15 seconds

Goal:

Build technical credibility.

Communicate engineering quality without overwhelming non-technical users.

---

## Final Call To Action

Expected engagement:

5–10 seconds

Goal:

Convert curiosity into action.

The final decision should feel natural rather than forced.

---

## Reading Rhythm

The page should alternate between:

High information density

↓

Visual breathing space

↓

Interaction

↓

Reflection

↓

Next discovery

This rhythm prevents fatigue while maintaining curiosity.

---

## Scroll Pace

Users should never encounter multiple major ideas within a single viewport.

Scrolling should reveal information progressively.

Each section should naturally encourage exploration of the next.

---

## Completion

A successful landing page should leave users feeling:

"I understand what this product does."

"I trust the quality."

"I want to explore further."

The page should end with curiosity rather than information overload.
